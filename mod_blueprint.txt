## 2. Core Architecture & Package Layout
- `com.powergrid.PowerGridMod`: Central registration (Blocks, Items, BlockEntityTypes, CreativeModeTabs, Capabilities).
- `com.powergrid.PowerGridClient`: Client-side initialization.
- `com.powergrid.grid.WireType`: Voltage tiers (LV: 400 FE/t, MV: 2,048 FE/t, HV: 16,384 FE/t), max distances, resistance loss rates.
- `com.powergrid.grid.WireConnection`: Endpoint representation for node-to-node overhead catenary wires.
- `com.powergrid.grid.IWireNode`: Universal interface for wire endpoints (connectors, relays, breakers).
- `com.powergrid.block`:
  - `TreatedWoodPoleBlock`, `SteelTrussPoleBlock`, `PoleCrossarmBlock` (overhead line infrastructure).
  - `WireConnectorBlock` (3 tiers: LV, MV, HV; interfaces directly with block energy capabilities).
  - `WireRelayBlock` (3 tiers: LV, MV, HV; pass-through junction nodes).
  - `TransformerBlock` (steps voltage tiers with realistic 98% efficiency).
  - `BreakerSwitchBlock` (redstone and manual inline circuit breaker).
  - `HeavyAccumulatorBlock` (industrial 4,000,000 FE capacitor bank).
  - `PowerPlantControllerBlock`, `GeneratorCasingBlock`, `AlternatorCoilBlock`, `TurbineRotorBlock`, `ExhaustStackBlock` (3x3x3 multiblock generator).
- `com.powergrid.block.entity`:
  - `WireConnectorBlockEntity`: Transfers FE to/from adjacent blocks and transmits power through connected wire lines.
  - `WireRelayBlockEntity`: High-density branch insulator.
  - `TransformerBlockEntity`: Substation step-up / step-down.
  - `BreakerSwitchBlockEntity`: Switched wire circuit.
  - `HeavyAccumulatorBlockEntity`: Buffer storage with auto-export.
  - `PowerPlantControllerBlockEntity`: 3x3x3 multiblock detection and 4,096 FE/t generation.
- `com.powergrid.item`:
  - `WireCoilItem` (LV, MV, HV): Right-click click-to-connect tool for overhead wire lines.
  - `VoltmeterItem`: Live diagnostic reader for voltage, FE buffer, throughput, and connection topology.
  - `EngineerHammerItem`: Multiblock assembly and block rotation.
  - `WireCuttersItem`: Clean wire severance.
  - Materials: `TreatedWoodItem`, `CopperWireItem`, `ElectrumWireItem`, `SteelCableItem`, `InsulatorItem`.

## 3. Technology Systems & Mechanics

### A. Voltage Tiers
- **LV (Low Voltage)**:
  - Wire: LV Copper Wire Coil
  - Max Rate: 400 FE/t
  - Max Distance: 16 blocks
  - Loss: 2.0% per block
  - Typical use: Local machine power, small generators, homesteads.
- **MV (Medium Voltage)**:
  - Wire: MV Electrum Wire Coil
  - Max Rate: 2,048 FE/t
  - Max Distance: 32 blocks
  - Loss: 1.0% per block
  - Typical use: Factory distribution, moderate distances, multiblock machinery.
- **HV (High Voltage)**:
  - Wire: HV Steel-Core Cable Coil
  - Max Rate: 16,384 FE/t
  - Max Distance: 64 blocks
  - Loss: 0.5% per block
  - Typical use: Long-distance transmission across biomes, heavy power plant exports.

### B. Multiblock Power Plant (3x3x3)
- Forms via the Grid Engineer's Hammer right-clicked on the front `PowerPlantControllerBlock`.
- Core: Center Alternator Coil or Turbine Rotor.
- Base: Generator Casing foundation.
- Exhaust: Top industrial stack.
- Generates 4,096 FE/t continuous industrial power with visible exhaust smoke and audio feedback.
